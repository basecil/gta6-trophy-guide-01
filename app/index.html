Sources & honesty

Names, descriptions and grades come from the official list as supplied (Exophase). PSN counters are carried from the supplied PSNProfiles notes. Every route, venue and method in a guide is our own advice read from the official description and has not been checked in the game; where a step is not pinned down, the guide says so.

No live trophy sites are contacted. The guide makes no requests beyond its own files, so it works offline once loaded.
